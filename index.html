Loma Verde Aquatic Center

1420 Loma Lane Chula Vista, CA 91911

Loma Verde has served the Chula Vista community since 1971, and reopened in 2023 after a full-facility renovation. It has a splash pad and two pools heated year-round, indoor changing rooms, family changing rooms and an event space. Every time on these pages comes from the pools' live schedule, so when the staff change it, these pages change with it.

Amenities

Competition Pool

  • Olympic-sized, 25 yards × 50 meters, 7–12.5 feet deep
  • 79–81°F
  • 22 short-course or 10 long-course lane lines, with backstroke flags
  • 25-yard, 25-meter or 30-meter water polo course with floating cages
  • Lap swim, deep water fitness, and team rentals: high school teams, a youth swim team and a youth water polo team

Recreation Pool

  • Instructional pool, 25 yards × 11 meters, 2.5–5 feet deep
  • 85–87°F
  • 5 short-course lanes with backstroke flags
  • Lap swim for casual or beginner swimmers, shallow water fitness, swim lessons, open swim and therapy rentals

Splash pad: 9 features and 24 fountains for children up to 12; adult supervision required.

Kickboards, pull buoys, water dumbbells, pool noodles, Coast Guard approved lifejackets and cubbies with combination locks are free to use. Team lockers, touch pads, a scoreboard/video screen and timing equipment can be rented. Digital pace clock, pool lift and ADA-accessible stairs, a shaded spectator area with bleachers, and chaise lounges and umbrellas.

What this replaces

  • "See online catalog for schedule and availability." The city page describes the pools but sends you to the Rec1 catalog for every time, and the catalog lists each pass and class separately. Here the whole center is one schedule.
  • A flyer from another season. The schedule PDF linked for Loma Verde is a Fall 2024 flyer. Here the times are the ones in the water this week.
  • "Pool space and lanes are not guaranteed." Lap swim passes don't say how much water is left when classes, lessons or team rentals are in. Here every booking holds its lanes, so you can see what's open.
  • Lesson times one listing at a time. Each level, day and time is its own catalog entry. Here every lesson block is on one calendar, by pool.
  • Team rentals out of sight. High school teams and the local youth swim and water polo teams rent the competition pool, but none of that is posted. Here it can be, so swimmers know before they drive over.
