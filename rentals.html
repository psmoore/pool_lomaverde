Reserving

Fill out the Application for Facility Use – Aquatics and bring it to the Loma Verde office when you make your reservation. Once the application is approved, secure your date with a $100 non-refundable payment toward your rental fees; the final payment is due 21 days before your event. Include setup and takedown times in your request. Tables and chairs are included.
